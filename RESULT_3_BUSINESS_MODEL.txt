**The "Founding 50" Outreach Strategy.**
Manual outreach on LinkedIn to high-ticket consultants you already know (or influencers you follow). Offer them a **"White-Glove Migration."** You build their KnotBio engine *for* them, free of charge, if they sign up for a $19/mo Elite Pro subscription.
- **Cost:** $0 (just your time).
- **Revenue:** $95/mo (from first 5).

## 2. THE 3 GROWTH REVENUE STREAMS
1.  **KnotBio Elite (SaaS):** The core $19/mo or $190/yr subscription. (Scalable).
2.  **KnotBio Agency (Managed):** $49/mo for agencies managing client funnels. (High LTV).
3.  **The "Authority Audit" (One-time):** A $497 deep-dive audit where you (or an AI agent) reviews their profile and copywriting for conversion optimization. (High Margin).

## 3. PRICING STRATEGY & PSYCHOLOGY
- **Free Forever:** 1 profile, standard blocks. (Anchor: Shows what they are *missing*).
- **Elite Pro ($19/mo):** The "Workhorse" tier. Priced exactly at the "No-Brainer" threshold for a business expense. It's less than a lunch, but protects a $25k brand image.
- **Agency ($49/mo):** Exploits the "White-label" desire. Agencies want to look like they have their own tech stack.

## 4. THE "IMMEDIATE YES" OFFER
**"The 1-Click Authority Switch."**
"We will migrate your current Linktree to KnotBio and optimize your copy using our Elite Framework—free of charge—when you join our annual plan today. 100% money-back guarantee if your lead count doesn't increase in 30 days."

## 5. COST STRUCTURE
- **Spend On:** Server stability (AWS/DigitalOcean), high-quality email automation (ConvertKit/Loops), and vCard generation API.
- **Avoid Spending On:** Office space, complex custom illustrations (use high-end photography/stock), and broad paid ads.
