# 🚀 SEO AND MOBILE BEST PRACTICES FOR ELITE SAAS

To ensure your Authority Engine is not only a conversion machine but also a search engine powerhouse, follow these technical and content best practices.

## 🔍 TECHNICAL SEO OPTIMIZATION

Our system handles the "heavy lifting" of SEO out of the box, but knowing how it works helps you stay ahead.

1. **Meta Data Mastery:**
   - Every profile generates unique **OpenGraph** and **Twitter Card** meta tags automatically.
   - We use your name, headline, and bio to populate these tags so your link looks premium when shared on LinkedIn, X, or Facebook.
2. **Structured Data (Schema.org):**
   - We inject **JSON-LD Structured Data** (Type: Person) into every profile.
   - This tells Google exactly who you are, what you do, and where you live online, helping you appear in "Knowledge Panels."
3. **Semantic Hierarchy:**
   - Ensure your profile title is your H1.
   - Use H2 and H3 for section titles within your custom blocks to help search engines understand the structure of your page.
4. **Canonical Links:**
   - We automatically set canonical URLs to prevent duplicate content issues, ensuring Google always indexes the primary version of your profile.

## 📱 MOBILE-FIRST UX & ACCESSIBILITY

In a world where 80% of traffic is mobile, a "good" mobile site isn't enough. It must be elite.

1. **The 44px Rule:**
   - All interactive elements (buttons, links, form fields) are styled with a minimum target size of 44x44px. This ensures "fat finger" friendly navigation.
2. **The 16px Font Rule:**
   - We enforce a minimum 16px font size for all mobile input fields. This prevents mobile browsers (especially iOS Safari) from automatically zooming in when a user taps a field, which breaks the UX flow.
3. **Frictionless Forms:**
   - Keep your lead forms short. Every extra field reduces conversion by up to 10%. Use our integrated forms for the fastest mobile experience.
4. **Accessibility (A11y):**
   - We use **ARIA labels** for icon-only buttons (like the hamburger menu) so screen readers can accurately describe the site to visually impaired users.

## 💡 USER TIPS FOR SEO SUCCESS
- **Keywords in Headline:** Include your primary niche keyword (e.g., "Business Coach" or "Real Estate Expert") in your profile headline.
- **High-Quality Avatar:** Use a clear, professional headshot. Search engines now index images and high-quality files boost your "trust" score.
- **Custom Domain:** Upgrade to the Pro plan to use a custom domain. Google favors root domains (links.yourbrand.com) over shared paths.
