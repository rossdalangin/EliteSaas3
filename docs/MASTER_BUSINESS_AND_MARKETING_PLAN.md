## 2. SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & TECHNICAL OPERATIONS
*Directives from the Chief Technology Officer (CTO) & Chief Product Officer (CPO)*

### A. The WordPress Hybrid Engine
KnotBio is engineered as a WordPress Theme (`saas-profile-theme`) and companion Lead Engine Plugin (`saas-profile-lead-engine`) hybrid to guarantee absolute database stability and high performance:
1.  **WordPress Theme (`saas-profile-theme`):** Renders mobile-first, lightweight, lightning-fast responsive public-facing profiles. Includes multiple pre-configured **Vibe Presets** (Midnight, Glassy, Luxury) that can be activated instantly.
2.  **Lead Engine Plugin (`saas-profile-lead-engine`):** Manages atomic custom post types (CPTs) for Profiles (`saas_profile`), Links/Blocks (`saas_link`), and Leads (`saas_lead`). Integrates automated analytical capture and custom hooks for webhook notifications.

### B. Elite Product Feature Matrix
Our technology stack features four proprietary, conversion-optimizing core engines:
*   **The Focus Center & Pulse Feed:** A real-time, consolidated administration dashboard showing a live activity stream of profile views, link clicks, and lead submissions.
*   **Integrated CRM & vCard Engine:** Users can build a lead form directly into their profile. Visitors can submit details with zero friction, download the user's customized contact card via the **vCard Exchange Loop**, or scan a dynamic QR code.
*   **Smart Scheduling & Visibility Control:** Content blocks can be scheduled to show and hide based on precise dates or hourly configurations (0-23) to match active marketing promotions.
*   **Native Cookie-Persistent A/B Split Testing:** Enables users to test different copy versions of their Call-To-Action (CTA) buttons, recording exact click-through performance metrics directly in our lightweight analytics event engine.
